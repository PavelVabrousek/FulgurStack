## Application changes by decision

- **Since ADR-159 (2026-11-08, local, UI only, awaiting the owner's manual test):** a folder added as lights, by drop or by Add light folder, gives itself when it holds frames directly; otherwise every folder below it (up to four levels) with frames of its own becomes a light set, and calibration-named folders are left out and listed. Frames from different folders are still never gathered into one set. The hint while dragging says what the drop gives, in red when nothing would be added, and the start screen card shows why nothing was added.
- **Since ADR-158 (2026-11-07, local):** the master's plate solve after a star registration also tries the frames' own field when the object or coordinates chosen in Settings name another target, and a chosen object that resolves nowhere falls back to the frames' OBJECT; a tried solve without a WCS is recorded in the report and the master's history. The Seestar S30 M 45 frames get their WCS with a stale "M 33" chosen.
- **Since ADR-157 (2026-11-06, local):** a calibration set filled kind by kind (+ Empty) is offered to light sets without one, as a scanned folder is; Set overview warns when a stacked light set has no calibration although a fitting set exists. Before, such a set was stacked uncalibrated without notice.
- **Since ADR-156 (2026-11-06, local):** a light set whose frames share no measurable stars with the reference, such as dense broadband L against narrowband OIII, is brought onto an anchor of its own set and linked to the reference through longer star lists; without a link it stacks as its own master only. On the Olyhen set the L master returns: 10/12 frames with star registration, 12/12 with sky registration.
- **Since ADR-155 (2026-11-05, local, awaiting the owner's PixInsight check):** a master gets its plate solution as FITS WCS (TAN with SIP distortion) in FITS and XISF: sky registration keeps its grid frame's solution, star registration solves its reference frame when a Gaia catalogue is set. The WCS is written only when the master's own stars match Gaia through it.
- **Since ADR-154 (2026-11-05, local):** a stack with plate solving on a quality measurement made without the bright-star lists only adds the lists; it no longer measures every frame again or discards the Use choices made in Frames.
- **Since ADR-144 to ADR-152 (2026-11-05, local):** ten optimization rounds on the 1,140-frame DS-A set, all masters byte-identical ([DS-A performance log](../reviews/M5_PERFORMANCE_DSA_20261105.md)). Applied: spans for distorted frames (ADR-144), ESD tests and sums four columns at once (ADR-146), parallel triangle fallback in star registration (ADR-148), parallel blind plate solve (ADR-149). Reverted after measurement: ADR-145, ADR-147, ADR-150, ADR-152; GPU analysis ADR-151. DS-A CPU 90.6 → 76.5 s, sky registration 108.5 → 83.7 s, LBN 437 110.8 → 91.3 s. An opt-in stage timeline (`FULGURSTACK_TIMELINE=1`) and a replay profiler support further work.
- **Since ADR-142 (2026-11-05, published, owner review PASS 2026-11-05):** the master and the diagnostic maps can be written as 32-bit float XISF instead of FITS: Settings › Output › Output format, or `--output master.xisf` in the CLI.
